About Us

Private engineering and field service support for energy-sector projects.

OCAN Energy and Projects Limited is a private engineering and field service firm based in Port Harcourt, Nigeria. The company operates within the broader oil, gas, and energy sectors, offering project management, petroleum engineering, facility maintenance, and safety management services. Beyond the energy sector, OCAN also delivers building construction, electrical wiring and maintenance, plumbing, and wall and floor finishing services including tiling and interlocking — giving clients a single, reliable partner for both technical field operations and physical infrastructure work.

OCAN’s value proposition is practical and field-tested: reliable delivery, safety-first execution, engineering judgment, clear supervision and a commitment to completing work on time.
